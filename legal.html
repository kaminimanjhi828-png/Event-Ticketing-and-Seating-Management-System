Legal Information

Important information about using Eventify.

Privacy Policy

At Eventify, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our platform.

Information such as your name, email address, phone number, and booking details may be collected when you create an account or make a booking.

This information is used to provide services, manage bookings, improve the platform, and communicate important updates.

Eventify does not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.

Terms & Conditions

By using Eventify, you agree to use the platform responsibly and provide accurate information when creating an account or making a booking.

Event information, dates, prices, and availability may change depending on the event organizer.

Users are responsible for reviewing event details before completing a booking.

Eventify reserves the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the platform when necessary.

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the individual event and its organizer's cancellation policy.

If an event is cancelled by the organizer, eligible ticket holders may receive a refund according to the applicable event policy.

Tickets that are voluntarily cancelled by users may not be refundable unless the event policy specifically allows cancellation.

Users should review the refund conditions before completing their booking.
